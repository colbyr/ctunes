I wanted a music player for my Plex library that works the way I like. This is it.

I'm the real audience here, but, if it suit you too, welcome aboard. Pay once and it's yours. No subscription, no ads, no tracking, and no server in the middle.

LOAD THE CHANGER
• Sign in with Plex and your server is found for you
• Browse albums, artists and playlists as a grid or a list, sorted by On Rotation, Recently Added, A to Z or Back Catalog
• One search across artists, albums, playlists and songs
• Album and artist pages tinted to match the cover art
• Heart tracks, and create, rename and reorder playlists

TAILOR TRACKS FOR WHO'S LISTENING
• Add a listener for each person you listen with
• Hide artists, albums or tracks for each listener
• Choose who's listening and the library adjusts, in the app and in the car
• Listeners and Shortcuts sync through your own iCloud account

QUICK MIX
• Pick some artists, albums or playlists, or nothing at all for the whole library, then Play, Shuffle or Mix Albums
• Save a mix as a Shortcut and play it from Home, a widget or Siri
• Shuffle spreads out artists and albums, so one record doesn't hog the queue

EVERYTHING ELSE
• CarPlay with On Rotation, Artists, Recently Added, Favorites and Playlists
• Download albums, playlists and favorites, in original quality or 320, 192 or 128 kbps
• Keep Favorites Downloaded, so they're always on hand
• No signal? Browse your saved library and play what's downloaded
• Ask Siri to play any artist, album, song or playlist, without opening the app
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for your Shortcuts
• Lock Screen, Control Center, headphone and AirPlay controls

RUNS ON
• iPhone, iPad and Mac

WHAT YOU NEED
• A Plex Media Server with a music library
• Listening away from home is a Plex requirement, not mine: your server needs Remote Access on, plus Plex Pass or a Remote Watch Pass
